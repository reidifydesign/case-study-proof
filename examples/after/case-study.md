# Fernbank Pottery: what we measured after a rebuild

Fernbank Pottery is a made-up ceramics shop. This is the same case study as the draft in examples/before, rewritten so that every figure points at dated evidence.

## What we did

We rebuilt the product pages and the checkout between April and May 2026.

## What we measured

Checkout completion went from 41% in March 2026 to 52% in June 2026 [E1].

Organic sessions went from 1,200 in March 2026 to 1,680 in June 2026, up 40% [E2].

Largest Contentful Paint on the product pages was 3.1s in March 2026 and 1.2s in June 2026 [E3].

The contact form received 38 enquiries in March 2026 and 51 in June 2026 [E4].

## What we cannot say

Checkout completion rose in the same weeks the new checkout went live. We did not test whether the new checkout caused the rise, and June included a seasonal sale.

The enquiry counts are small, so a change of a few either way would not be unusual.
